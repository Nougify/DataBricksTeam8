The insight is that each hub needs a different fix. Park Royal and Waterfront have spare capacity in the early morning and late evening, so simply retiming trips closes most of the gap at no cost. UBC’s peak buses are over capacity, so rescheduling can’t help; about 112 more weekday departures at midday, about 5% more service, cuts underserved visits from 20% to 3.4%.
