The what-if planner lets a planner add buses, or move them at zero cost, hour by hour, and see the gap close live. It never moves a bus if that would push the busiest line past 85% full.

Hub Pulse · Rogers × Databricks Hackathon · Vancouver · 8 / 11

Everything is Databricks-native: files in a Unity Catalog volume, SQL pipelines into Delta, AI functions for the forecast and the written recommendations, a Genie space for plain-English questions, an AI/BI dashboard, and an AppKit Databricks App for the what-if planner. The planner’s capacity rule: an hour can only give up buses while its busiest line stays at or under 85% full, assuming its riders spread over the buses that remain.
