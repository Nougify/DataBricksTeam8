An origin has a one-seat ride if a line serving the hub also stops within 1 km of it by bus, or 2 km of a SkyTrain, SeaBus or West Coast Express station. Surrey, Richmond, New Westminster and North Vancouver alone are 38% of UBC’s regional visitors, and every one of those trips needs a transfer onto UBC’s already crowded lines.
