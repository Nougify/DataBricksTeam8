The problem

At UBC, visits peak at 1 p.m. The buses peak at 8 a.m.

Visits: 12–2 p.m. holds 17% of the day’s visits but only 10% of departures.

Departures: 7–9 a.m. gets 16% of the day’s buses for 7% of visits.
